How to get your hangar data

EasyHangar shows everything in your own RSI account: ships, pledges and buy-backs. Getting your data takes three steps. You set up step 1 once; after that it is one click.

  1. Add the EasyHangar button to your bookmarks bar. If you cannot see a bookmarks bar under the address box, press Ctrl+Shift+B (Cmd+Shift+B on a Mac). Then hold the mouse button down on the blue button below and drag it up onto the bar.
    EasyHangar Drag me to the bookmarks bar
  2. Open your hangar on the RSI website and click that bookmark. Open my RSI hangar Sign in if asked, then click the EasyHangar bookmark. A small panel appears in the corner: press Start and wait. With hundreds of buy-backs it takes several minutes.
  3. Come back here. When the panel says Done, your hangar appears on this page. If it does not, press Show my hangar in the panel.

Where is my data kept? Only on your computer. A copy is saved as a file named like EasyHangar-2026-11-13.json in your browser's Downloads folder (Ctrl+J shows it), and this page remembers your hangar in this browser. To open a saved file later, press Load a file at the top.

Bookmark not working? Then on the RSI page press F12, open the Console tab, paste and press Enter. Chrome and Edge may first ask you to type allow pasting.

Nothing is uploaded anywhere. EasyHangar only reads your own hangar and buy-back pages, changes nothing in your account, and never sees your password.
